# Neem & Nest store policies

## Shipping
We ship across India. Orders are dispatched within 1-2 working days. Metro cities usually receive orders in 2-4 days and other locations in 4-7 days. Shipping is free on orders above 499. Orders below 499 have a shipping fee of 49.

## Cash on delivery (COD)
Cash on delivery is available on orders up to 2,000. A COD handling fee of 29 applies. If a COD order is refused or not accepted after 3 delivery attempts, it is returned to origin (RTO) and future orders on the same number may need prepaid payment.

## Returns and exchanges
Unopened, unused products can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Opened skincare and haircare products cannot be returned for hygiene reasons, unless the product arrived damaged, leaked, or was the wrong item. For damaged or wrong items, share a photo within 48 hours of delivery and we will send a free replacement or a full refund.

## Refunds
Refunds for prepaid orders go back to the original payment method within 5-7 working days after the return is received or the order is cancelled. Refunds for COD orders are paid by bank transfer or UPI within 5-7 working days after we receive the customer's details.

## Order cancellation
Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are shipped. Once an order is shipped, it cannot be cancelled, but it can be returned under the returns policy.

## Changing address or phone number
The delivery address or phone number can be changed only before the order is shipped. After shipping, contact support and we will try to update it with the courier, but this is not guaranteed.

## Product safety and allergies
All products are dermatologically tested. Do a patch test before first use. For skin conditions, allergies, pregnancy or medical questions, consult a dermatologist or doctor; our support team cannot give medical advice.
